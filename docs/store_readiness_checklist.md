# Store Readiness Checklist

## Play Store Listing
- App icon final (512x512).
- Feature graphic final (1024x500).
- Screenshot portrait minimal 6 screen.
- Short description dan full description.
- Demo video URL (opsional tapi direkomendasikan).

## Compliance
- Privacy Policy URL live.
- Terms of Service URL/live page.
- Data Safety form terisi sesuai implementasi.
- Content rating questionnaire selesai.

## Release Process
- Internal testing pass.
- Closed beta minimal 10 tester.
- Bug P0/P1 ditutup sebelum production.
- Staged rollout: 5% -> 20% -> 100%.

## Monitoring
- Crash-free users > 99.5%.
- Rating >= 4.3.
- Feedback masuk dan ditriase maksimal 24 jam.
